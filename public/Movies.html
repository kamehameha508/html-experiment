    1. The Dark Knight(2008)


    Synopsis

    This Legendary film was Directed by Chirstopher Nolan. Starring Christian Bale as Bruce Wayne/Batman, and Heath Ledger as The Joker, makes for the greatest Superhero movie of all time, and arguably one of the best films of all time as well. It follows Batman's story as how his acts gather the interest of Joker, a psycho who takes pleasure in human suffering and does the things for the thrill of doing it. It also follows Bruce Wayne's personal life, which takes a sharp turn when the lady he loves, Rachel, gets proposed to by Harvey Dent, the Gotham Judge. This causes Harvey to lower his guard and ultimately Joker manages to kill off Rachel and burn away half of Harvey's face. Blinded by an unfathomable amount of love for Rachel and the will to kill anyone who failed in saving her, he turns on his own ideals, becoming 'Two-Face' from the once revered 'Gotham's White Knight'.

    Review

    Overall Score - 9.5/10

    The movie has the perfect balance of everything. Drama, action, and thrill. The moral dilemma faced by Lucius Fox near the climax makes the movie feel more human, which is missing in today's cinema. The music by James and Hans Zimmer takes it to an entirely new state.
